Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This policy explains how TMN Decorating & Maintenance uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They are widely used to keep websites working, remember preferences and understand how sites are used.

2. The cookies we use — strictly necessary only

We keep things deliberately minimal. We use only strictly necessary cookies, which are required for the client portal to function:

  • access_token — keeps you securely signed in to the client portal. It is a secure, http-only session cookie that lasts around 15 minutes.
  • refresh_token — a secure, http-only cookie that quietly renews your sign-in so you don't have to keep logging back in. It lasts up to 30 days, or until you sign out.

These cookies are set only when you sign in or register, they never track you across other websites, and they contain no advertising identifiers.

3. What we don't use

  • No advertising or remarketing cookies
  • No third-party analytics or social-media tracking cookies
  • No consent-banner cookies — because there is nothing to opt into beyond the sign-in cookies described above

4. Local storage and similar technologies

The colour visualiser may temporarily use your browser's memory to process the photo you are working on. Photos you upload for visualising are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers unless you save a look to your account.

5. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time (usually under "Privacy and security"). Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent the client portal from keeping you signed in, but the public pages of the website will still work normally.
