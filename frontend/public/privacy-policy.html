Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

TMN Decorating & Maintenance ("we", "us", "our") is a painting, decorating and property maintenance business based in Plymouth, UK. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website and our client portal, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. We act as the data controller for this information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address and a securely hashed password when you register for the client portal.
  • Enquiries and messages — anything you send us through the portal chat, including photographs of your property, and your replies to us by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Project information — quotes, jobs, appointments, site notes and colour choices (including looks you save in our colour visualiser and colours you favourite).
  • Invoicing details — the name, address and billing information shown on your invoices. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details.
  • Technical data — anonymous page-view counts used to understand how the site is used.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide quotes, carry out work and manage your projects (performance of a contract).
  • To operate your portal account — sign-in, saved looks, favourites, chat and invoices (performance of a contract).
  • To send service emails such as invoices and receipts, and to reply to your enquiries (performance of a contract / legitimate interests).
  • To keep records required by tax and consumer law (legal obligation).
  • To improve the website and our services (legitimate interests).

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who we share data with

Only the providers needed to run the service: Stripe (secure card payments), our email delivery provider (transactional emails such as invoices), and our website hosting provider. Each processes data on our behalf under data-protection agreements. We never share your data for anyone else's marketing.

4. How long we keep it

Client and project records (including invoices) are kept for six years after the end of the financial year of the work, as required by UK tax law. Portal accounts and chat history are kept while you remain a client; you may ask us to delete your account at any time.

5. Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask for a copy of your data, corrections, deletion, restriction of processing, or a portable copy, and you can withdraw consent where we rely on it. Email info@tmndecorating.co.uk and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

6. Security

Passwords are stored only as irreversible bcrypt hashes, sign-in sessions use secure, http-only cookies, and access to client data is limited to TMN Decorating & Maintenance staff.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above. Significant changes will be announced on the website.
